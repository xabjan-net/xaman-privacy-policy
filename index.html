Privacy Policy

Xaman — XRPL Account Sync

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Xaman — XRPL Account Sync is a browser extension designed to help users access and manage information related to their XRP Ledger (XRPL) accounts. This policy explains how information is accessed, used, and handled by the extension.

2. Information We Access

The extension may access public XRPL account addresses, account balances, publicly available transaction data, and basic extension preferences required for its functionality.

The extension does not request users' recovery phrases, private keys, or wallet passwords.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to display account details, monitor XRP balances, retrieve public blockchain information, and provide account activity features.

4. Data Storage

Basic extension settings and preferences may be stored locally using Chrome storage functionality. Public blockchain information is retrieved through supported XRPL network endpoints.

5. Data Sharing

The extension does not sell or rent user information. Requests to XRPL network endpoints or infrastructure providers may include public account addresses and technical connection information necessary to retrieve blockchain data.

6. Tracking and Advertising

The extension does not use account information for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking.

7. User Control

Users can remove locally stored extension settings by clearing the extension's stored data or uninstalling the extension. Public blockchain records cannot be deleted by the extension.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored settings remain until removed by the user or the extension. Public blockchain records are maintained by the XRP Ledger network.

9. Children's Privacy

The extension is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
